## Music in this book

_Art Cure_ explores the many positive the arts have on various dimensions of health. The author has a music background, so naturally, music features heavily in this book. Most of these tracks had personal significance to the subjects of illustrative stories throughout the book. A few were references to research studies, and a couple served as chapter titles, such as “Help”, “Fix You”, and “I Will Survive”.

## Music in this book

In “Martyr”, music helps the characters navigate and connect with the world and the people around theme. In particular, the album “Mama’s Gun” by Erykah Badu marks the formation of a central relationship. Another character recounts experiencing a spiritual awakening on each replay of Allegri’s “Miserere”.
